## Historical evidence

- Timeline/map range is approximately 10000 BCE–1912 CE, starting with prehistoric settlements. Early archaeological sites are learning nodes; they do not assert political borders. There is no year zero. Use `toOrdinal` / `fromOrdinal` for calculations. Xia has no reliable upstream boundary: retain archaeological/textual nodes and the explicit limitation. Shang/Zhou polygons have coarse source dates; do not relabel Shang geometry as Zhou to fill the 1046–1001 BCE gap. Ancient `Later Zhou` is the Zhou royal domain, distinct from the Five Dynasties polity. Early `dateLabel` and `endYear` preserve approximate/ranged dates rather than asserting an exact year.
- Navigation periods and representative years are learning aids. Actual dynasty dates live in `src/data/dynasties.ts`; do not equate source map intervals with a dynasty's existence.
- Never claim the collection is complete. Read `public/data/coverage.json` and `data/research/content-audit.json` before making coverage claims. Basic identity records are not full biographies.
- Every person, event and dynasty needs an introduction and source links. Events also need background, impact, date and geographic uncertainty where applicable.
- Related events do not prove personal presence. Do not generate travel from event coordinates, a ruler's territorial extent, administrative orders, poems, or modern roads.
- Only independently checked dated visits belong in `src/data/journeys.ts`. Same-year records retain source order; repeated cities must remain distinct records.
- The currently verified journeys are Su Shi, Kangxi and Zhuge Liang. Kangxi did not personally visit Taiwan or Nerchinsk; the battle at Zhaomodo was fought by Feiyanggu's force. Zhuge Liang's residence at Longzhong uses the Xiangyang interpretation and must retain its controversy note.
- Journey animation interpolates between records as a reading aid. It is not an assertion of a route, speed, or location during an unrecorded year.
- Conflicting or unknown dates stay unknown. Do not invent a year or add birth/death events merely to inflate event counts.
- A person who died before an event needs an explicit explanation of the indirect/posthumous relationship or must be removed from that event.
